About Stillframe

Stillframe is a curated collection of free desktop wallpapers. Every wallpaper is available in 2K or 4K, so it stays sharp on laptops, monitors and large displays.

What you will find

Our collection is organised into categories: Anime, Modern, Motivational, Nature, Superhero and Abstract. Most pieces use a thick, textured impasto-painting look, with visible palette-knife strokes and rich colour.

How it works

  • Browse the collection or pick a category.
  • Open a wallpaper to see its resolution, quality and description.
  • Select Download Wallpaper to save the original file. No account is needed.

Our goal

We want to offer a small, carefully chosen library rather than thousands of random images. New wallpapers are added regularly. Stillframe is free to use and supported by advertising.
